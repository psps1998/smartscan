Privacy Policy

How Smartscan, a product of Smear Technologies ("we", "us"), collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. What Smartscan is for

Smartscan is a household grocery and inventory tracker. You scan a purchase invoice (a photo or PDF), review what was read, and save it. Smartscan then keeps your household's stock, spending and consumption reports up to date and suggests what to buy next. We use your information only to provide and improve that service.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address (which is your User ID), family name, and a password. The password is never stored as typed; we keep only a salted one-way hash (scrypt).
  • Household details you enter: number of persons, adults and children, gender and age-group counts, buyer members, language, currency and your item categories and budgets.
  • Invoice and stock data: the lines you save from an invoice (items, quantities, prices, tax, discounts, vendor, date, buyer), your inventory, and the consumption you record.
  • Uploaded invoice files: the image or PDF you scan is processed in memory to read it and is not kept on our servers after the scan completes.
  • Messages you send us: feedback and Contact us submissions (name, email, category, remarks).
  • Technical data: your IP address, used for abuse prevention (rate limits), and to guess your country at signup so we can preset language, currency and starter categories. A session cookie keeps you logged in.

We do not ask for payment card details, government IDs, contacts or precise location.

2a. Cookies

We use one essential cookie (ss_session) to keep you logged in. It is HttpOnly, SameSite and, on the live site, Secure, and expires after 7 days or when you log out. The contact form uses Google reCAPTCHA, which may set its own cookies and collect device and browser signals to tell people from bots. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and to send activation and password-reset emails.
  • To read your invoices, maintain your inventory, and produce your stock, usage, overstock, pattern and next-order reports.
  • To reply to your messages and improve Smartscan.
  • To prevent abuse, spam and fraud, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf, only to run Smartscan:

  • Anthropic (Claude): when you scan an invoice, the file is sent to Anthropic's API to read its contents. We do not send your account details with it.
  • Amazon Web Services: hosting of the application and database (Asia Pacific, Mumbai region), encrypted backups, and email delivery (Amazon SES).
  • Google reCAPTCHA: protects the Contact us form from bots.
  • An IP-location service (ipwho.is): receives your IP address at signup to detect your country.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights and users. If Smartscan is ever merged or acquired, your data may transfer to the new owner under this same policy.

5. How we protect your data

  • All traffic to smartscan.in is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes. Changing your password signs you out on every device.
  • Each household's data is kept separate: every request is tied to your account and cannot read another household's records.
  • Activation and reset links are single-use, expire (24 hours and 1 hour), and are stored only as hashes.
  • Login attempts and public forms are rate-limited to slow down guessing and spam.
  • The database is backed up regularly to private cloud storage. Access to the server and the admin tools is restricted to authorised staff.

No system is completely secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and household data while your account is active. When an account is deleted, we remove its household, buyers, categories, inventory, invoices and settings. Encrypted backups containing it are overwritten on their normal rotation. Contact and feedback messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and improve the service.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your household, buyers, categories, inventory and invoices in the app, and change your password and settings at any time. You may also ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account and its data. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws elsewhere. To use any of these rights, write to support@smartscan.in from your account email and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

Smartscan is for adults managing a household. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. Household counts and age groups you enter describe your family only in aggregate; we do not store names or details of children.

9. International use

Our servers are in India (AWS Mumbai). If you use Smartscan from another country, your information is transferred to and processed in India, and, for invoice scanning, by Anthropic in the countries where it operates.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Smartscan changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes we will notify you in the app or by email.

11. Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email info@smartscan.in or support@smartscan.in, or use the Contact us form on smartscan.in.
